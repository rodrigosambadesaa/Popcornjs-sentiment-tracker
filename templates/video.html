02 / METHODOLOGY

What the numbers mean

Polarity is estimated using the VADER English-language lexicon. Scores describe words in the transcript—not what the speaker, audience, or viewers actually felt. The historic transcript contains transcription errors, and its timestamps came from an earlier video recording; manual alignment may be needed.
